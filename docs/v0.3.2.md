# v0.3.2 — Decision Engine Calibration

## Boundaries and contracts

`/api/snapshot` remains schema **0.2**, unchanged. `/api/decision-support` remains **0.3**, extended with `model_version=decision-0.3.2`, `feature_version=weekly-features-2`, `data_through_week`, `generated_at`, per-player `model`, `adjusted_matchup_difficulty`, and `team_strength_v2`. Existing actual matchups, production, raw defensive splits, eligibility, roster structure and transactions remain available. The old `team_strength` contract remains alongside v2.

Football quality, Start Value, football acquisition estimate, legal transaction marginal value, Pickup Rating, and market value are distinct. No trade generation. Market value is null. K/DST/IDP have availability/schedule context but no advanced acquisition model. Platform eligibility remains authoritative, including WR/DB; provider positions remain provenance. Ambiguous reception premiums remain unknown rather than guessed.

## Exact formulas and policy

Let `Q` be sample-blended historical league-scored PPG, `R` last-three-game PPG, `X` last-two-game covered-rule xFP, and `O` the position-specific opportunity score divided by 100 and multiplied by 25 for QB, 20 otherwise.

* `Q = (n × current PPG + k × prior PPG)/(n+k)`. Prior is the last eight recorded games of the immediately preceding season; `k=4`, reduced to `1` for changed team or established role expansion. No prior means `k=0`. A new two-game role is provisional, not an automatic ROS reset. Current sample, prior contribution, reason and confidence are public.
* All positional **Start Value** models selected `[Q,R,X,O]` weights `[0.6,0.2,0.2,0]` on validation MAE. Availability for this week is separate; bye, injury or kickoff passed makes `weekly_start_value=null`, not a false zero projection.
* **Football acquisition** models selected the same blend for QB/RB/WR; TE selected `[0.3,0.1,0.4,0.2]` on validation three-week rank correlation. Missing predictors explicitly fall back to Q; coverage and fallback are exposed. No Q means no forecast. The final Pickup Rating does not redistribute missing weights.
* Opportunity component contribution is `weight × clamp(input/scale,0,1)`. Inputs use the latest two recorded games on the current team. Missing components contribute no points and retain missing coverage. Exact weights/scales are in `lib/decision/model-config.js` (pairs below are weight/scale):

| Position | Opportunity components |
|---|---|
| RB | snap share 20/.8; carry share 20/.7; carries 15/20; targets 10/6; target share 5/.2; xFP 25/20; red-zone opportunities 5/4 |
| WR | snap share 15/.9; targets 20/10; target share 20/.3; air yards 5/100; air share 10/.4; WOPR 10/.8; xFP 15/20; red-zone targets 5/3 |
| TE | snap share 15/.8; targets 20/8; target share 20/.25; air yards 5/70; air share 10/.3; WOPR 10/.7; xFP 15/18; red-zone targets 5/3 |
| QB | attempts+sacks 45/40; carries 20/8; xFP 25/25; team plays 10/70 |

Red-zone and QB team-play inputs remain missing in the live model; they are not manufactured or reweighted. Attempts+sacks excludes scrambles and is labeled accordingly. Efficiency (FPOE, RACR, EPA) has **zero direct decision weight**. FPOE does not promise mean reversion. xFP and actual/FPOE use the same covered league rules; cumulative and per-game values/history are retained.

**Replacement:** `0.7 × mean(best three available quality values) + 0.3 × population demand cutoff`. Demand rank is `ceil(teams × eligible starter demand × (1 + bench slots / skill starter slots))`. FLEX demand is split among eligible positions; Superflex assigns QB structural demand. Cutoffs beyond known population use its last observed value and expose rank/population size; this is censored evidence, not known replacement talent. If either baseline is missing, the available baseline is returned with partial status. Dual eligibility takes the best single VOR, never a sum. League scoring, including TE premium, is applied before valuation. Baselines describe acquisition assets; they are not healthy-only weekly streaming baselines.

**Legal add/drop:** active capacity counts starter+BN slots; reserve/taxi players cannot free an active slot. Full rosters require one eligible drop, vacancies permit null drop, over-capacity rosters are withheld. Each supported candidate is evaluated against each conservative drop. A Hungarian assignment solves before/after lineups, maximizes filled supported slots then model value, and uses each player once. Unknown compatible players or unverifiable started-game lineup locks withhold marginal weekly value.

`net = starter change + 0.25 × bench VOR change + 0.10 × (candidate VOR − dropped VOR)`.

Bench VOR sums positive VOR of unassigned active assets, including injury insurance; starter gains and depth losses remain signed. Output retains the best three pairs, dropped asset cost, before/after lineup, protected assets and evaluated pair count. Non-positive best pairs are diagnostic, not recommended. The 0.25/0.10 weights are transparent policy, **not transaction-level backtest calibration**. Drops protect injury/PUP/IR assets, missing-value assets, expanding-role stashes, RBs with ≥20% snaps or ≥10% carry share, Superflex QBs, upper-15%-of-position quality assets, K/DST/IDP and players whose kickoff passed. This intentionally errs toward retaining assets; meaningful handcuffs need not be proven future starters. Commissioner locks, deadlines, reserve eligibility and multi-move transactions are not verified/executed.

**Pickup Rating:** 40% positional percentile of calibrated football acquisition estimate; 15% position opportunity; 35% `clamp(net×10,0,100)`; 5% role evidence (75 established expansion, 60 provisional, 40 observed but not expanding); 5% available-player Sleeper add-interest percentile. Missing components stay missing, including nested opportunity/forecast coverage. Evidence groups include coverage signatures. Ratings require football evidence and ≥55% coverage; positive legal net is required for a ranked transaction. One-week matchup is **0%**, retained as context until incremental predictive benefit is tested. The old v0.3.1 heuristic is preserved in `waiver-score-v031.js` for comparison.

**Immediate upgrade:** available scheduled player, starter improvement ≥3, net ≥1, ≥3 scored games, ≥70% evidence coverage, role score ≥55, and data through the prior week. This conservative configuration is not presented as a learned breakpoint. A +1–2 gain or weak role cannot pass. Injury stashes retain a separate category; provisional expansion remains explicitly small-sample.

## Schedule adjustment and uncertainty

For each defense/position/game, compare actual points to the sum of those opponents' own mean production in **other games against other defenses**, using only weeks before the decision cutoff. Omit the meeting if any opposing-player baseline is missing. Average the residuals and shrink by `n/(n+6)`. Adjusted FPA is league mean positional allowed PPG plus the shrunk residual; rank descending for “most favorable.” Preserve raw totals, raw PPG, residuals, shrinkage, covered games, per-game evidence, through-week, scoring profile and recent-four-game residual. This is our transparent residual model, not proprietary 4for4 aFPA. It is contextual, not yet added to the calibrated forecast. In-season leave-defense-out reconstruction can use later games **only if earlier than the decision cutoff**; a historical decision never sees its target week.

Four or more scored games permit observed 20th/50th/80th percentiles and standard deviation, labeled descriptive, not forecast confidence intervals. “Moderate” evidence requires ≥8 games and ≥80% role coverage; otherwise low. Historical range coverage is reported, not assumed. Statistical-appearance, offensive-snap and ≥10-snap participation PPG have separate numerators/denominators. Active games and true DNPs remain unknown; absent rows/byes never become zero scores.

## Backtest protocol and results

Run `npm run backtest` (or `node scripts/backtest.js`). Raw downloads cache in ignored `.data/backtest`; the tracked `artifacts/backtests/v032-results.json` includes source URLs, SHA-256 hashes, samples, metrics, format/position/year slices and every validation candidate. `--write-calibration` is an explicit opt-in to regenerate production weights.

2021 supplies priors only; 2022 is the development/reference season; 2023 selects a small prespecified interpretable blend grid; 2024 and 2025 are untouched evaluation seasons. Weeks 3–17 use only earlier-week features. Candidate membership uses past activity, never current Sleeper ownership or future scores. Evaluate next observed weekly output and 1/3/4-calendar-week mean output, with at least two recorded outcomes for longer horizons. Missing future rows are censored, not DNP zeroes. Tuning uses PPR only; TE premium is evaluated without retuning. Preserved v0.3.1 **observable components** compare 20% PPG percentile +10% recent percentile +20% targets+carries percentile; historical roster, trend and matchup terms are unavailable and not fabricated.

|---|---:|---:|
| PPR next-week samples | 4,058 | 4,121 |
| Start MAE / PPG baseline | 4.773 / 4.856 | 4.721 / 4.779 |
| Start weekly-position Spearman | .555 | .539 |
| Start top-N hit rate | .560 | .526 |
| Correct non-tied start/sit pairs | .719 | .720 |

| Acquisition MAE / PPG baseline | 3.503 / 3.659 | 3.390 / 3.554 |
| TE-premium Start MAE / PPG baseline | 4.913 / 5.002 | 4.863 / 4.921 |

Usage correlations with next-week output were consistently stronger than efficiency: snap share .537/.566, target share .527/.543, carry share .597/.648, WOPR .541/.558, xFP .507/.479, versus FPOE .064/.062, RACR .017/.013 and QB EPA proxy .131/.088. These are univariate screens with different position/sample coverage, **not causal effects or directly comparable feature importances**. Recent production and xFP improve the tested blend; extra role weight was selected only for TE acquisition. The opportunity submodel remains interpretable policy, not a separately fitted production forecast. Future-xFP feature screens and confidence buckets are in the artifact.

Important limits: corrected retrospective source records are not archived publication-time vintages; ffopportunity v1.0.0 documents 2006–2020 training but its archive was re-uploaded later. Source hashes and the version pin prevent silent model-version drift, not proof of historical publishing latency. No precise historical waiver pools/injury states exist here, so no claim of verified league-transaction win rate. The small error improvement does not establish a large competitive edge. Serially overlapping multiweek outcomes are not independent trials; no significance claims are made.

## Secondary foundations and deferrals

* `team_strength_v2` exposes unique legal starters, positional starter strength, bench VOR, needs/surpluses, injury/bye exposure, Superflex/TE-premium context and null market value. Legacy strength remains available. Count-based needs are an early contract, not trade advice.
* `robustConsensus` accepts weekly/ROS horizon, source/expert metadata, family, scoring and publication date. Latest independent-family observations enter a freshness-weighted median (three-day half-life, 14-day max); optional accuracy weights require ≥100 historical games, a pre-publication cutoff and bounded .75–1.25 influence. Missing players are excluded. Redistribution permission is explicit. Legacy arithmetic `average_rank` is retained solely for API compatibility; no production decision score consumes it. No rankings feed is scraped or redistributed.
* Recommendation observation/storage ports are append-only, content-addressed, model/version/time-aware and idempotent. They capture returned recommendations, components, coverage and transactions, with truncation scope disclosed. Durable storage is **not enabled**. Free options: local JSONL archival, or a private GitHub Actions artifact workflow with explicit retention/permissions. Do not rely on Vercel's ephemeral filesystem; choosing a durable provider/retention policy remains separate work.
* Tested PBP import derives red-zone carries/targets, inside-10/5 carries and goal-line carry share with game/play provenance, deduplication and exclusions. End-zone targets remain null. **Live PBP ingestion is deferred**: full-season raw files should be precomputed outside a serverless request, not loaded into every dashboard request. The current endpoint reports these inputs missing. No PBP/betting fields enter live scoring.
* External weekly/ROS ranks/projections/ownership and ownership history remain unavailable. No invented market value. Full EPA/dropback including scrambles, success rate, job security/durability and calibrated floor/ceiling probabilities remain unsupported. Unsupported league rules include `st_ff` and `st_fum_rec`; DEF/IDP scoring remains unsupported. Seven current statistical identities are unmatched rather than name-guessed.

Production review also protects assets with fewer than four current scored games and no prior-season baseline. This prevents a weak two-game sample from turning a rookie or previously unestablished upside asset into an automatic drop; it does not claim to identify rookies from missing records. Decisions/observations also include the public deployment commit as `implementation_revision` where Vercel supplies it, so policy fixes within a release remain distinguishable.

Before v0.4: archive real recommendation observations; validate transaction opportunity costs and thresholds prospectively; precompute allowlisted PBP metrics; test incremental schedule adjustments out of sample; add allowed market observations; handle platform lock/reserve workflows and multi-move drops; examine historical data-vintage and DNP selection bias. Trade generation remains deferred.
