# v0.3.1 football judgment and evidence

The original snapshot schema remains **0.2**. The decision endpoint remains **0.3**, with additive analytics, category and value-profile fields. No trade generator, paid provider, HTML scraper or persistent ownership database is introduced. Sleeper remains authoritative for league state and actual scores.

## Eligibility and scoring

`lib/normalize/positions.js` preserves every Sleeper fantasy position. A WR/DB can appear in both legal tabs, but is evaluated once and can fill only one starting slot. League starter settings determine available positions. Existing six snapshot position keys remain present; applicable IDP keys can be added. Provider positions never grant platform eligibility and remain separately visible. FB is an analytics RB grouping, not an edit to platform eligibility.

Historical points use league coefficients, including the TE reception premium. A single platform offensive position controls reception-premium eligibility if a provider disagrees. Where multiple offensive positions imply different premium treatment, points are unknown with an ambiguity warning. D/ST and IDP scoring remain unsupported. Configured leagues still have unsupported `st_ff` and `st_fum_rec`; supported-rule PPG is explicitly partial. PPG counts statistical appearances, not assumed games played or fabricated zero-action appearances.

## Source architecture and provenance

Existing nflverse weekly statistics and ID adapters are reused. New adapters are under `lib/sources/usage`:

| Source | Verified published data | Usage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [nflverse snap counts](https://nflreadr.nflverse.com/reference/load_snap_counts.html) | `nflverse-data/releases/download/snap_counts/snap_counts_2026.csv` | Offensive snaps and snap share, joined through PFR IDs |
| [nflverse player statistics](https://nflreadr.nflverse.com/reference/load_player_stats) | Current-season weekly player CSV | Targets, carries, air yards, target/air-yard shares, WOPR, RACR and EPA aggregates |
| [ffopportunity](https://nflreadr.nflverse.com/reference/load_ff_opportunity.html) | `ffverse/ffopportunity/releases/download/latest-data/ep_weekly_2026.csv` | Expected event counts, re-scored using league rules |

Snaps originate with PFR and are consumed from nflverse's published dataset; this app does not scrape PFR. The ffopportunity model is an external model, not independently calibrated by this app. No generic provider fantasy-point total is substituted for league scoring. These community feeds can be delayed, incomplete or revised. Adapters validate columns, bound downloads and timeout, cache in memory, and fail independently. Source timestamps and through-week metadata remain visible. Invalid/missing source IDs and ambiguous mappings are never matched by guessed names. Unmapped snap-row counts include non-fantasy linemen and defenders and are not a count of missing fantasy assets.

## Analytics contracts

`player_context[id].analytics` has all prior regular-season weekly history, recent trends, season means and per-metric observation counts. A missing observation stays null. Recorded zero snaps stay zero; missing target rows do not become zero targets. Carry share divides by all recorded team carries, including QB runs. Opportunity volume is targets plus carries, not a claim of equal fantasy value per event.

Expected opportunity exposes average covered-rule xFP and matched-rule fantasy points over expected, modeled game count and through week. Fumbles, special teams, kicking, IDP and unsupported custom rules are excluded, not set to zero. Each modeled game's components retain `[actual_points, expected_points, raw_actual, raw_expected, league_coefficient]`, described by `opportunity_contract.component_columns`. Model actuals and expectations use the same rules. The average covers modeled games only, with sample size shown; it is not a weekly projection.

`team_environment` preserves weekly passing/rushing EPA and attempt/sack/carry counts. Its EPA/play denominator is recorded attempts + sacks + carries. Pass rate means attempts-or-sacks divided by those recorded plays. QB passing EPA per attempt-or-sack excludes scrambles; it is deliberately not called full EPA/dropback. Full play-by-play success rate and red-zone opportunities remain null because a bounded, tested play-by-play pipeline is not part of this release. EPA is environment context, not a large pickup-score factor.

## Conservative signals

Role signals require four current recorded games on the same team, latest observation through the prior week, and no more than a four-week span (allowing a bye). They compare the latest two with the preceding two; a missing value blocks that metric's signal.

| Flag | Trigger |
| --- | --- |
| Role expanding / contracting | Snap share changes by at least 15 percentage points |
| Target share rising | Increase of at least 6 percentage points |
| Backfield takeover watch | RB carry share increases by at least 15 percentage points |
| Production lagging opportunity | Target-share increase of 6 points with flat/falling fantasy production |
| Production below opportunity | Four modeled games; recent covered xFP at least 10/game and matched-rule FPOE at most -4/game |
| Efficiency warning | Four-game evidence; recent RACR above 1.5 and at least 30 air yards in each recent game |
| High-value receiving usage | Recent WOPR at least 0.6 with four-game evidence |

Each flag exposes its inputs, windows, threshold and sample. They are descriptive screens, not breakout probabilities or validated forecasts. At release, only two completed games are available: strong trend/contingent claims are intentionally absent.

## Roster-specific Pickup Rating

`lineupEvaluator` computes the best legal historical-PPG lineup before/after adding a candidate. It reuses dynamic-programming states across the full eligible pool, respects FLEX/SUPER_FLEX/IDP slot eligibility, and uses each player once. IR/taxi/out players do not enter the current active lineup. Unknown compatible roster players block a claimed upgrade. Unsupported slots or more than 16 starter slots return unknown rather than inventing an assignment.

Depth value measures improvement above the stronger of the best compatible bench player and a league-depth replacement cutoff. The cutoff is the population PPG at `ceil(league_size × fractional_position_slot_demand × (1 + bench_slots / offensive_starter_slots))`. Flexible demand is split equally among eligible positions. This is an explicit approximation of scarcity, not a learned market price. An 8-team pool can therefore have a substantially higher replacement bar than a 12-team pool. A missing population cutoff stays unknown. The model evaluates an addition; it does **not** optimize which player to drop from a full roster.

| Component | Weight | Evidence |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Player quality proxy | 20% | Historical league-scored PPG percentile; ROS rank unavailable |
| Role / opportunity | 20% | Recent targets + carries positional percentile |
| Marginal roster improvement | 30% | Capped score: `10 × starter_gain + 3 × depth_gain` |
| Upside / role expansion | 10% | Four-game snap-share change |
| Recent production | 10% | Recent PPG positional percentile |
| Near-term matchup | 5% | Upcoming positional matchup; shrunk toward neutral through six games |
| Market interest | 5% | Sleeper available-player add-interest percentile |

Percentiles consider every eligible position; a player uses their best eligible percentile. Missing weights are never redistributed. At least 45% evidence plus season/recent production is required for a rating. `coverage_percent`, `comparable_group`, component scores, weights, contributions and raw inputs are returned. Results are grouped by evidence coverage and exact component set before score; a higher displayed group is not a claim of higher football value. Compare ratings only within the same component set. Legacy `score`, recommendations and limited candidates remain, while `pickup_rating` can also describe injury stashes.

The targets/carries opportunity component does not apply to K/DEF/IDP. For QBs it measures rushing involvement, not passing volume; passing production and environment are separate. Starter-strength totals include only known slots and expose a completeness flag.

Six independently selected categories are evaluated across the full pool before each is truncated: immediate upgrades, best overall adds, upside stashes, injury/PUP/IR stashes, contingent RB watch, and short-term matchup plays. Contingent watch requires four-game carry-share evidence and an observed same-team lead; it never claims a verified depth-chart successor. Short-term matchup plays require at least four opponent games and a positive legal-lineup gain. Categories can overlap. Category membership references `waivers.candidate_details` by player ID; counts/omissions remain explicit. Position filters apply to each returned category's top 20, not a second full-pool query.

## Sleeper interest

The top-100 upstream feed is censored. An unlisted player's add count is unknown, not zero. An observed player's available-pool percentile/rank uses that league's full eligible pool, with competition ties and separate percentiles for each platform position. Unlisted players are below observed players for this ordering. Raw observed counts remain accessible. This is Sleeper add interest, never ownership percentage, ownership history or usage growth.

## API size and future profiles

Repeated model definitions and category documents are shared while all weekly evidence is retained. HTTP gzip is negotiated for large decision responses with `Vary: Accept-Encoding`; browsers and normal API clients decode transparently. Clients explicitly disabling compression can encounter serverless response-size limits late in the season; use gzip. A simulated 17-game history is covered by transport tests and manual size checks.

Sleeper's large player catalog bypasses Next's 2 MB data-cache limit and instead uses a 24-hour, promise-coalescing cache per warm server process. Cold instances still fetch the source; failures are not cached. This corrects repeated oversized-cache warnings without changing the snapshot schema.

`PlayerValueProfile` retains platform/provider identity, historical football-value basis, null ROS/market values and separate roster-value evidence. `TeamStrengthProfile` adds known starter strength, completeness, bench depth, replacement cutoffs, fractional position demand and eligible-count needs/surpluses. Positional count surpluses can overlap for dual-eligible players and must not be summed as unique assets. Full lineup evidence remains in team/waiver profiles. No trade generation is implemented.

Run `pnpm test`, `pnpm build`, `pnpm test:ui`, and `pnpm smoke:live`. Set `SMOKE_BASE_URL` to verify deployed endpoints. Smoke tests assert both configured formats (10-team Superflex/TE premium and 12-team PPR), source availability, sample safeguards, actual matchup, normalized eligibility, and unchanged snapshot schema. They do not mutate Sleeper state.

Before v0.4: backtest heuristic weights and category thresholds; add legal add/drop optimization; resolve zero-action participation and rare special-teams scoring; introduce tested play-by-play red-zone/success-rate aggregation; validate true ROS/market observations from a permitted provider; consider paginated player analytics and full-pool position/category queries. Ownership history and trade generation remain deferred.
